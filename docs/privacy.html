OpenBanners Overlay – Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

OpenBanners Overlay is an open-source Android companion app for doing Ingress mission banners (source code). It has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics. Apart from the optional settings backup described below, the developer does not collect, store or receive any personal data.

Location

While the overlay is running, the app uses your device's precise location to draw your position on the route map and to tick off mission steps you have reached. Your location is processed only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any other party, and it is not stored after the overlay is closed.

Network requests

  • Bannergress API (api.bannergress.com): when you share a banner or mission link with the app, it downloads that banner's public mission data. The request contains the banner or mission ID, not your location. Bannergress is a separate service with its own privacy terms; like any web server it can see your IP address.
  • OpenBanners (openbanners.org): when you open a Mission Day plan link, the app downloads that plan and marks missions done in it. The request contains the plan ID, not your location.
  • Ingress: the Next button opens the next mission in the Ingress app through a link.ingress.com link. What happens there is governed by Niantic's privacy policy.

Settings backup on OpenBanners (optional)

Settings → Back up to OpenBanners saves the app's settings (overlay, mission card, route map and compass options) with your Bannergress account on openbanners.org, replacing any earlier backup; Restore from OpenBanners reads it back and shows what would change before anything is changed. Your location, progress and permissions are not part of the backup.

For this the app opens openbanners.org in your browser, where you sign in with Bannergress and tap Allow. The website then gives the app a one-time code, which the app exchanges for access to its own backup only, for 15 minutes. The app never receives or stores your Bannergress sign-in, and it keeps nothing afterwards. The backup is covered by the OpenBanners privacy page: you can delete it, or download everything OpenBanners stores for your account, in ⋮ → Settings on openbanners.org.

Other permissions

  • Display over other apps: to show the route map and controls on top of Ingress.
  • Notifications: to show progress and when a mission step is in range.
  • Clipboard (optional, off by default): copies the current mission number for passphrase missions. It stays on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
