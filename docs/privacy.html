OpenBanners Overlay – Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

OpenBanners Overlay is an open-source Android companion app for doing Ingress mission banners (source code). It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect, store or receive any personal data.

Location

While the overlay is running, the app uses your device's precise location to draw your position on the route map and to tick off mission steps you have reached. Your location is processed only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any other party, and it is not stored after the overlay is closed.

Network requests

  • Bannergress API (api.bannergress.com): when you share a banner or mission link with the app, it downloads that banner's public mission data. The request contains the banner or mission ID, not your location. Bannergress is a separate service with its own privacy terms; like any web server it can see your IP address.
  • Ingress: the Next button opens the next mission in the Ingress app through a link.ingress.com link. What happens there is governed by Niantic's privacy policy.

Other permissions

  • Display over other apps: to show the route map and controls on top of Ingress.
  • Notifications: to show progress and when a mission step is in range.
  • Clipboard (optional, off by default): copies the current mission number for passphrase missions. It stays on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes and contact
